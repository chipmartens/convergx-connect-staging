Role What they carry

What to check when there is no list

Rigour

You cannot check a list that does not exist. Check the review instead.

ConvergX does not publish who is in the room.

A buyer states a requirement on the understanding that it does not leave the review, and a published list of buyers would end that on the first day.

So the thing worth checking is the review, and it runs at both ends.

ConvergX vets you before you go in front of anyone. ConvergX decides who is on the other side of the introduction it makes.

One organisation is answerable for both halves, to you as much as to them.

The same review runs on them. ConvergX asks a buyer what it is actually able to take on, in the same way it asks you.

That is what makes an hour of your week worth spending here. You are not a name somebody scanned off a list. Somebody read a requirement and decided it was you.

The review runs on both sides. The buyer whose requirement you would answer went through the same door you are asking to come through, with the same organisation standing at it.
