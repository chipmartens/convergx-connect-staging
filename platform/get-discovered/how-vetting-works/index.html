Vetting is not for sale.

Nothing anyone pays ConvergX changes an assessment. Every applicant is asked the same things, and a failed check stays failed.

Vetted, not scraped. ConvergX reads what you submit, looks at what you have already delivered, and decides.

A problem holder is never put in front of a company that did not clear the review, and there is no version of this where writing a cheque skips it.

The vetting review, both sides of it
What gets checked What will not happen
Who the company is. Legal entity, ownership, and where you operate. ConvergX does not resell, repackage or list what you submit.
What you have built, and where it is running today. No sponsorship, table or referral advances an application. There is no version of the review that skips a step.
Whether the capability you claim is the capability you hold. A check you do not clear is not reopened by anything except new evidence about the company.
Whether the work is already commercialised, at TRL 8 to 9. Early-stage and research work goes to a separate ConvergX arm, not around this review.
Certifications and accreditations, recorded as you state them. ConvergX issues no certification and validates none. What you state is what is recorded.
What else the company is carrying, and who is still there from the work you cite. ConvergX rates nobody and scores nobody. There is no number to move.

ConvergX does not sell a place in the room. A failed check is not a queue position, and there is no route around it.
