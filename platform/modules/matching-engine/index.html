What it does

Candidates that could answer a requirement get gathered into a shortlist.

The shortlist goes in front of a person at ConvergX. It goes nowhere else, and neither side of a possible introduction ever sees it.

A match exists when that person picks a counterparty off the list. Nothing generates a match.

Where it stops
