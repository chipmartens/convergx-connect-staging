01

The document goes in exactly as you wrote it

You submit the requirements document exactly as written, and a person at ConvergX reads it and fills the fields. Do not include controlled, export-restricted or classified technical data in what you send.

  • No form to fill in first
  • Nobody retypes a line

02

What comes back is a set of fields

The requirement comes back broken into fields covering the scope, the standard it has to meet, and the date it is needed by, which is the form a requirement has to be in before it can be compared with anything.

  • Scope, standard and date
  • Structured for comparison

03

Fields can be held against a capability record

Fields are what allow a requirement to be held against a company's capability record. An attachment can only be forwarded to somebody else to read and form an opinion about.

  • An attachment cannot be compared
  • Judgement still sits with a person

Common questions

Questions
Do I have to rewrite my requirements document?

No. You hand over the document you already wrote, and it comes back as fields covering what is needed, to what standard, and by when.

Who reads the document I send?

A person at ConvergX reads it. It is not passed to any other company, and what other attendees are served is the fields, with no author attached.

What happens to the original?

It stays attached. The fields are how the requirement gets compared against a capability record, and the original remains available alongside them.

Does the platform judge the requirement?

No. Structuring a requirement is separate from assessing it, and what it is worth and who should be shown it stays with a person at ConvergX.
