A requirements document arrives as fields, not as an attachment.

A requirement that lives inside a PDF can be read by a person and by nothing else.

What it does

Stating the requirement

You hand over the document you already wrote. Nobody has to rewrite it into a form first.

What comes out is the requirement broken into fields: what is needed, to what standard, by when.

Fields are the reason the same requirement can be compared against a capability record at all. An attachment cannot be compared with anything.

Where it stops

Scope

Structuring a requirement is not judging it. What the fields are worth, and who should see them, stays with a person at ConvergX.
