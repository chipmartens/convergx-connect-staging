Fifteen modules, grouped by what they are for.

The groups follow the order the work happens in: a requirement is stated, a person decides, an introduction happens and leaves a record.

Matching at the Congress is done by ConvergX, by hand, on purpose.

Stating the requirement

01 / Requirement

Getting a requirement into the system with no name on it, and getting requirements in from outside.

Opportunity Board

Posted requirements, carrying no author to anyone who reads them. ConvergX can decline a post and take it off the board.

RFP Intake and Analysis

A requirements document taken in as structured fields rather than an attachment.

Tender Scraping

Public tender notices collected for review.

RFP Authoring

Drafting a requirements document inside the platform.

Deciding who should meet

02 / Decision

Material a person at ConvergX reads before deciding.

Supplier Scoring

Capability records scored against the requirement they are being considered for.

Matching Engine

Candidate shortlists put in front of the person who decides.

Configurable Scoring

Weights a ConvergX administrator could set.

The introduction and its record

03 / Introduction

Moving an introduction through its steps, and keeping what came of it.

Procurement Workflow Engine

Configurable steps between a posted requirement and an introduction.

Role-Gated Portals

Separate views for problem holders, solution providers and ConvergX staff.

Document Request Workflow

Documents requested and tracked between two parties.

Attribution Mechanism

The Congress

04 / Congress

The three days themselves, and the phone in the room.

Congress App

Agenda, sessions and the board on a delegate's phone. Push notifications reach Android only.

Event Management

Registration, session scheduling and delegate logistics for the event itself.

Reference

05 / Reference

Material the platform can be asked about.

Knowledge-Base Chat

Questions answered from ConvergX reference material.

Trade Agreements Library

Reference material on the agreements that govern cross-border work.
