Keep the broker joined to the introduction

The introduction and the party who brokered it are recorded together at the moment it happens, rather than reconstructed afterwards from whoever has the clearest memory of that week.

02

Attach the outcome to what actually started it

Work that comes out of an introduction can be attached to the introduction itself, rather than to whoever tells the story best at the next conference. Credit follows the record.

  • Outcome joins the introduction
  • Not whoever tells it best

03

Produce the record long after the meeting

It persists, so it can be produced months afterwards and not only on the day. This is what makes ConvergX's accountability checkable rather than simply asserted on a website.

  • Persists past the engagement
  • Checkable, not asserted
