Common questions

Configurable Scoring
Who sets the weights?

A ConvergX administrator, per sector and per kind of work. Buyers and suppliers do not set them and cannot see them.

Can I see why a company scored the way it did?

The weights are visible to the person who set them, so a result can be explained by pointing at what produced it.

Is this the match standard?

No. What makes a good match sits in a person's head and has never been written down. Weighting is a different thing, and writing the standard down is an open design problem.
