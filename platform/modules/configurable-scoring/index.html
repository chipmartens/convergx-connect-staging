Deciding who should meet

What the scoring weighs is set by a person, not baked in.

What matters in aerospace is not what matters in agriculture, and a fixed formula pretends otherwise.

Request access

What it does

A ConvergX administrator sets what counts and how much, per sector and per kind of work.

The weights are visible to the person setting them, so a scoring result can be explained by pointing at what produced it.

Changing what is weighed changes what the shortlist surfaces, which is the point.

Where it stops

Buyers and suppliers do not set these weights and cannot see them. Weighting is not the match standard: what makes a good match still sits in a person's head and has never been written down.

Common questions

Who sets the weights?

A ConvergX administrator, per sector and per kind of work. Buyers and suppliers do not set them and cannot see them.

Can I see why a company scored the way it did?

The weights are visible to the person who set them, so a result can be explained by pointing at what produced it.

Is this the match standard?

No. What makes a good match sits in a person's head and has never been written down. Weighting is a different thing, and writing the standard down is an open design problem.
