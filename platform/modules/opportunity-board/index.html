Common questions

Opportunity Board
Who can see that the post is mine?

ConvergX can. Nobody else. The post reaches ConvergX with your name and your email on it, because somebody has to be able to ask you about it, and every other attendee is served the requirement with no author field at all.

Can I take a post down?

Yes, and so can ConvergX. A post that should not be on the board comes off it.

Does posting commit me to anything?

No. A post is a requirement stated. Nothing becomes a meeting until a person at ConvergX decides it should, and both sides consent before an introduction happens.
