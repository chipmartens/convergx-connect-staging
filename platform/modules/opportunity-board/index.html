Stating the requirement

A requirement goes up without your name on it.

The board carries the requirement to everyone in the room. It carries the author to nobody.

Request access

What it does

You post what you need. The post goes up when you submit it, and no queue holds it back.

Every other attendee reads the requirement with no author field on it. Not a masked one and not a hidden one.

The post reaches ConvergX with your name and your email attached, because somebody has to be able to answer questions about it.

ConvergX can decline a post, which takes it off the board.

Where it stops

The board is where a requirement is stated, not where it is answered. Nothing on it becomes an introduction until a person at ConvergX decides it should.

Common questions

Who can see that the post is mine?

ConvergX can. Nobody else. The post reaches ConvergX with your name and your email on it, because somebody has to be able to ask you about it, and every other attendee is served the requirement with no author field at all.

Can I take a post down?

Yes, and so can ConvergX. A post that should not be on the board comes off it.

Does posting commit me to anything?

No. A post is a requirement stated. Nothing becomes a meeting until a person at ConvergX decides it should, and both sides consent before an introduction happens.
