02

Ask the room without telling the room it is you

Everyone reading the board gets the requirement and no author. There is no masked name and no hidden field to uncover, because your identity is not in what other attendees are served at all.

  • No author field on the attendee view
  • Nothing to uncover later

03

Let ConvergX hold who is asking, so somebody can answer

Your name and email travel with the post to ConvergX and stop there. Somebody has to be able to come back to you with questions before an introduction is worth making, and that somebody is a named person.

  • Your details reach ConvergX only
  • A named person can come back to you

04

Take a post down the moment it stops being true

Requirements change and budgets get pulled. You can withdraw a post, and ConvergX can decline one, which removes it from the board for everybody rather than leaving a dead requirement in the room.

  • Withdraw it at any time
  • ConvergX can decline a post

Common questions

Questions
Who can see that the post is mine?

ConvergX can. Nobody else. The post reaches ConvergX with your name and your email on it, because somebody has to be able to ask you about it, and every other attendee is served the requirement with no author field at all.

Can I take a post down?

Yes, and so can ConvergX. A post that should not be on the board comes off it.

Does posting commit me to anything?

No. A post is a requirement stated. Nothing becomes a meeting until a person at ConvergX decides it should, and both sides consent before an introduction happens.
