Know exactly whose desk an introduction is sitting on

An introduction is a sequence and not a moment: a post goes up, a person reads it, a counterparty is picked, both sides consent. Every step is written down with an owner on it, so when something stalls the question of who has it has an answer.

01

Write the sequence down with an owner on each step

The steps between a requirement going up and two parties meeting are set out in order, each with a named owner, rather than living as an assumption about who is supposed to do what next.

  • Steps in order, owner on each
  • No assumption about who has it

02

Fit the sequence to the sector it runs in

A defence introduction and an agriculture introduction do not need the same steps, so the sequence is configurable. Export control and clearance belong in one of them and not the other.

  • Configurable per sector
  • Defence and agriculture differ

03

See what a stalled introduction is waiting on

Where a step is waiting on somebody, it is visible that it is waiting and on whom. Nothing advances on its own, and the last gate in every sequence is always ConvergX.

  • Waiting steps are visible
  • The last gate is always ConvergX
