Chase certifications without chasing them over email

Qualification runs on paperwork: certifications, quality approvals, insurance, export documents. Asking for one is a record here rather than a message, so what was requested and what is still outstanding stays visible to both sides at once.

Ask for a document and have the asking on the record

One side requests a document and the request becomes a record rather than an email somebody has to remember sending, or go hunting for in a thread six months after the fact.

02

Show both sides what is still outstanding

What was asked for, what came back and what is still missing is visible to both parties at once, so neither side is guessing about whether the ball is currently in their court.

  • Visible to both parties
  • Outstanding stays outstanding

03

Answer what was provided, months afterwards

The record stays after the engagement, so the question of what was actually provided has an answer that is not somebody's inbox. Nothing here judges whether a document is good enough.

  • Persists past the engagement
  • Nothing here judges a document
