What it does

Scoring runs against one requirement, so one company scores differently on the next.

What comes out is material a person at ConvergX reads before deciding. It is an input to that reading, not a substitute for it.

A score does not travel outside the sector it was earned in.

Where it stops

Nothing here decides anything. A score cannot rule a company in or out, and no score ever reaches either side of an introduction.

Common questions

Does a score decide anything?

No. A score is material a person at ConvergX reads before deciding. It cannot rule a company in or out.

Does the other side see its score?

No score reaches either side of an introduction.

Does a score follow a company between sectors?

No. Scoring runs against one requirement at a time and does not travel outside the sector it was earned in.
