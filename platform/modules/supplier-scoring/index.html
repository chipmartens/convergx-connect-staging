Supplier Scoring

Score a company against this job, not in general

Capability records get scored against the one requirement they are being considered for, so a company that fits this job may not fit the next one. What comes out is material a person at ConvergX reads before deciding who should answer your requirement.

Request access

01

Score against this requirement, not in the abstract

A company is not strong or weak in general. Scoring runs against one requirement at a time, which is why the same company can score well for this piece of work and poorly for the next.

  • One requirement at a time
  • A different score on the next job

02

Give the person deciding something to read

A score cannot rule a company in or out. It is material a person at ConvergX reads before making the call, and it is an input to that reading rather than a replacement for it.

  • An input, never a decision
  • Read by a person at ConvergX

03

Keep a score inside the sector that earned it

Past performance in aerospace says little about fitness for an agriculture programme, so a score does not travel outside the sector it was earned in. Neither side of an introduction is shown one.

  • No score travels between sectors
  • Never shown to either side

Common questions

Supplier Scoring
Does a score decide anything?

No. A score is material a person at ConvergX reads before deciding. It cannot rule a company in or out.

Does the other side see its score?

No score reaches either side of an introduction.

Does a score follow a company between sectors?

No. Scoring runs against one requirement at a time and does not travel outside the sector it was earned in.
