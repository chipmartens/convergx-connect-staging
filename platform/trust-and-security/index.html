How the system behaves

02 / Behaviour

Identity moves on consent. Nothing that identifies one party reaches the other until both have agreed to the introduction.

The attendee view of the opportunity board carries no author field. Not a masked one, not a hidden one. ConvergX staff see the submitter and nobody else does.

What a role can see is enforced by the application itself, not asserted by the identity provider that signed the user in.

The record is the artefact

03 / The record

You can ask for the record of an introduction and be shown it.

What it holds is set out on Vetting and introductions. What matters to a reviewer is that it persists, so it can be produced months afterwards and not only on the day.

That is the part you produce when somebody asks why this company and not another one.

Audit history on every entity is in build and not production-hardened.
