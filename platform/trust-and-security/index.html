ConvergX Connect holds no security certification.

No SOC 2 report, no ISO certificate, no FedRAMP authorisation, no CMMC or CPCSC level, no authority to operate.

Read this page as description, not as assurance

01 / Not claimed

No third-party security audit is on record for this platform.

Everything below describes how the system behaves today. None of it is a warranty. Where a behaviour is load-bearing for your review, ask ConvergX to demonstrate it before you rely on it.

How the system behaves

02 / Behaviour

Identity moves on consent. Nothing that identifies one party reaches the other until both have agreed to the introduction.

There is no self-signup. An account exists because ConvergX created it after reviewing an application.

What a role can see is enforced by the application itself, not asserted by the identity provider that signed the user in.

Deleting a record removes it from view. The record itself stays.

The record is the artefact

03 / The record

What a reviewer can ask for is the record of an introduction: who asked, who agreed, and when.

It stays after the introduction is made, so it can be produced later and not only at the time.

Audit history on every entity is in build. It is demonstrable today and it is not production-hardened.
