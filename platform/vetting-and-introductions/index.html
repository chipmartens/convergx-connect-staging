The gate is on the introduction.

A requirement reaches the board the moment it is submitted. Nothing becomes a meeting until a person at ConvergX makes it one.

What the software actually does

01 / Today

No attendee ever sees an author. Not on a post marked anonymous, and not on any of the others either.

A submitted requirement goes up straight away. What other attendees are served has no author field in it at all.

ConvergX sees the name and the email behind every post, and can decline one, which takes it off the board.

A match exists when a person at ConvergX picks the counterparty off a list. Nothing generates it.

Neither side learns who the other is until ConvergX releases it, and the consent of each sits on the record.

What the record holds

02 / The record

The requirement, the consent of each side, and the introduction between them.

It stays after the two parties have met, so who put them together does not depend on anyone remembering.

In build

03 / Not September

The reasoning behind a decision sits outside the platform today.

In build, demonstrable, not hardened: the written reason a candidate was ruled in or out, kept instead of dropped when the shortlist narrows.

It is not in the September scope.
