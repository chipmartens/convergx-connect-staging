So your name is not on the ask.

The board carries no author to another attendee on any post, whether or not it was flagged anonymous. Your identity moves once, when you agree to an introduction.

Why the gap stays unsaid

A capability gap is a statement you make in front of the people who sell into it.

Raise it in a hallway and you have told whoever was standing there.

Put it in a public solicitation and you have disclosed it to the market, on a procurement clock, with the programme attached.

Either route costs you the disclosure up front, before it has produced anything at all, and a disclosure does not come back.

What everyone else can see

Confidentiality ends at the introduction
