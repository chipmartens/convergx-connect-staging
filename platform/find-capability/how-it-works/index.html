Nothing moves until a person decides.

Your post goes up with no name on it. A person at ConvergX reads it and decides whether an introduction follows.

Both gates sit after the post rather than in front of it.

What you write is one sentence about a gap, read by ConvergX and nobody else. The gate that matters is on who ends up in a room with whom.

A person reads the requirement, and the decision goes on the record. ConvergX promises no timing and no match.

ConvergX does the matching by hand, deliberately.

A person reads the requirement and decides who should see it, and that decision is what ConvergX provides.

A decision produced automatically would leave nobody answerable for it, which is the difference this arrangement exists to hold.
