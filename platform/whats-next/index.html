The job, not the feature list

01 / Destination

The intent is that a requirement raised in a week with no Congress in it reaches the same decision. It is not built.

The Congress runs three days. The requirements it surfaces outlast the room, and carrying them between one Congress and the next is currently a person's week.

So the work ahead is a volume problem, not a judgement problem. Hold the same standard when there are more requirements than one room can hold. Software that speeds up a decision nobody is answerable for is not the thing being built.

What no version of this removes

02 / Boundary

A person decides who meets, in every version of this.

Nothing planned decides on its own, and nothing planned makes an introduction without ConvergX approving it first.

If a shortlist is ever generated, it is a shortlist somebody then has to defend. ConvergX stays the party answerable for the introduction, and that is the constraint every planned capability is designed around.

What makes a good match has not been written down.

Scoring weights are not configurable, and the standard a candidate is scored against exists in a person's head rather than in a document.

That is the design problem we are working on. There is no shipped mechanism for it today.

Saying so here is cheaper than you finding it in a pilot, and a roadmap that names nothing it cannot do yet is a roadmap somebody wrote for the wrong reader.

If what you need is the version that runs between the Congresses, say so now. ConvergX will tell you what exists today and what does not.
