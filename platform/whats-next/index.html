What a company last told ConvergX stays current between one Congress and the next. Not built yet.

02 / Boundary

A person decides who meets, in every version of this.

Nothing planned decides on its own, and nothing planned releases one party's identity to the other without a person at ConvergX doing it.

If a shortlist is ever generated, it is a shortlist somebody then has to defend.

ConvergX stays the party answerable for the introduction, and that is the constraint every planned capability is designed around.

What makes a good match has not been written down.

The match standard sits in a person's head. It has never been written down, and scoring weights are not configurable.

It is a different thing from the vetting standard, which is written out in full on How we vet.

Writing it down is the open design problem. No shipped mechanism exists for it today.

Saying so here is cheaper than you finding it in a pilot.

If what you need is the version that runs between the Congresses, say so now. ConvergX will tell you what exists today and what does not.
