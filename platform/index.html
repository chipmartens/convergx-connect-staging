The software

Attendees see requirements. ConvergX sees the requirements and the people behind them.

A requirement reaches the opportunity board the moment it is submitted. It carries no author to anyone reading it, and ConvergX can decline it and take it down.

ConvergX staff see the name and the email. They pick the counterparty by hand, off a list, and neither side learns who the other is until ConvergX releases it.

A supplier portal reads records your organisation already holds. This board holds the requirements those records have no answer for.

Every module carries the build state it is actually in, where it is listed, and most of them have nothing to do with September.

Where it stops

Scope

No invoicing, no purchase orders, no payment matching, no contract management. ConvergX Connect stops at the award decision.

Your finance system keeps the transaction and your contract system keeps the contract. Nothing gets ripped out to run this, which is why it can be tried on one requirement.
