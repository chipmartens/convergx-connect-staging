Where it stops

Scope

No invoicing, no purchase orders, no payment matching, no contract management. ConvergX Connect never touches the transaction.

Your finance system keeps the transaction and your contract system keeps the contract. Nothing gets ripped out to run this, which is why it can be tried on one requirement.

ConvergX Connect holds no security certification.

Trust and security

No SOC 2 report, no ISO certificate, no FedRAMP authorisation, no CMMC or CPCSC level, no authority to operate.

How the system behaves

Behaviour

Identity moves on consent. Nothing that identifies one party reaches the other until both have agreed to the introduction.

The attendee view of the opportunity board carries no author field. Not a masked one, not a hidden one. The submitter reaches ConvergX and nobody else.

What a role can see is enforced by the application itself, not asserted by the identity provider that signed the user in.

Deleting a record removes it from view. The record itself stays.

The record is the artefact

The record

You can ask for the record of an introduction and be shown it.

What it holds is set out on Vetting and introductions. What matters to a reviewer is that it persists, so it can be produced months afterwards and not only on the day.

That is the part you produce when somebody asks why this company and not another one.

Audit history runs on every entity.

What the software actually does

Today

No attendee ever sees an author. Not on a post marked anonymous, and not on any of the others either.

A submitted requirement goes up straight away. What other attendees are served has no author field in it at all.

A post reaches ConvergX with its author's name and email attached, and ConvergX can decline it, which takes it off the board.

A match exists when a person at ConvergX picks the counterparty off a list. Nothing generates it.

Neither side learns who the other is until ConvergX releases it, and the consent of each sits on the record.

What the record holds

The record

The requirement, the consent of each side, and the introduction between them. Who decided, and when.

It stays after the two parties have met, so who put them together does not depend on anyone remembering.
