How access gets decided

The decision

ConvergX decides who gets in, and the decision runs in both directions.

The doors carry separate names because the questions behind them are not the same. A problem holder is asked what the requirement is and where it is already sourced. A capability holder is asked what they have built and where it is already running.

Where it stops

The boundary

ConvergX ConneX does not handle invoicing, purchase orders, payment matching or contract management, and it never touches the transaction itself.

Your ERP, your supplier portal and your sourcing workflow stay where they are. Because nothing is replaced, there is no migration to fund and no incumbent system to defend.
