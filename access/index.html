How access gets decided

Access / 01

ConvergX decides who gets in, on both sides, one request at a time.

The two doors have different names because the questions behind them are different. A problem holder is asked what the requirement is and where it is already sourced. A capability holder is asked what they have built and where it is already running.

ConvergX reads what comes in and answers. No response time is promised here, because none can be.

Where it stops

Access / 02

No invoicing, no purchase orders, no payment matching, no contract management. ConvergX Connect stops at the award decision.

That boundary is why starting is a bounded thing. Nothing in your existing stack is replaced, so there is no migration to fund and no incumbent system to defend.
