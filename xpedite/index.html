ConvergX Capital is a venture capital firm, and Xpedite is its bridge financing service.

Xpedite is available on a transaction ConvergX has brokered.

ConvergX names financing as one of three conditions that stop a working solution from entering a new industry. The last mile

Xpedite addresses that third condition.

Between the date a buyer commits to a transaction and the date funds reach the supplier there is an interval, and one of the two counterparties has to carry it. On a cross-sector transaction that interval is where a transaction is most likely to fail, because the counterparties have no trading history with one another and neither has an established basis for extending credit to the other.

Who it is for

The buyer that has committed, and the supplier that has been awarded the work.

The buyer. The decision has been taken and the expenditure has been approved, and the funds still have to move through an internal process that was built for control rather than for speed.

The supplier. The work has been awarded and the supplier has been asked to begin, and beginning consumes capital before any of it is recovered.

Bridge financing covers the interval between two dates.

How it works

The date the counterparties agree, and the date the funds move.

In general usage, a bridge facility is short-term capital advanced against a payment that is expected to arrive. The word bridge is used literally. The interval is temporary, both counterparties expect it to close, and something has to carry it while it remains open. No duration is published for an Xpedite facility.

The instrument itself is long established and there is nothing novel in it. What is particular to Xpedite is the population of transactions it applies to, which are transactions ConvergX has brokered.

ConvergX Capital is a bespoke venture capital firm, which is to say that a transaction is structured on its own terms rather than issued under a standard programme, and the terms of any individual structure are not published. The description is a statement about the firm and not about the source of funds on a given facility. Whether a facility is advanced by ConvergX Capital or syndicated to other parties is not published, and the two arrangements carry different obligations.

The amount, the pricing, the duration and any security attaching to a facility are matters for ConvergX Capital and the counterparty. None of them is published and none of them is stated on this page.
