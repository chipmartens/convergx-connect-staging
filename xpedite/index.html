The decision is made. The money has not moved.

Xpedite is the bridge financing service from ConvergX Capital, for companies on a deal ConvergX already brokered.

ConvergX names financing as one of three things that stall a proven solution on its way into a new industry. The last mile

Xpedite is the answer to that one.

Between the day a buyer decides and the day money reaches the supplier there is a stretch somebody has to carry. On a cross-sector deal that stretch is where things break, because neither company has worked with the other before and neither one has a reason to extend the other any rope.

The buyer that decided, and the supplier that won the work.

The buyer. The decision is made and the spend is approved, and the money still has to move through a process built for control rather than for speed.

The supplier. The work has been won and it is being asked to start, and starting costs money before any of it comes back.

How it works

The one everyone agrees on, and the one the money moves on.

Bridge financing is what covers the distance between them. The word bridge is literal. The gap is temporary, everybody involved expects it to close, and something has to span it while it is open.

That is the whole idea, and it is old. What is new here is whose deals it is pointed at.

How ConvergX spans the gap is the part nobody outside ConvergX has been told. Putting up your own money and arranging somebody else's are different businesses, with different obligations attached, and ConvergX has named neither.
