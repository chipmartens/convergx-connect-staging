The position

Aerospace and defence, agriculture, construction, energy, manufacturing, military, mining and natural resources, technology. ConvergX convenes them together. The Congress has run for ten-plus years.

It sits in each of them as the organiser, never as a participant.

That is the only position from which one industry can be asked about another, and the only one with nothing riding on the answer.

The four are not adjacent markets. They qualify differently, buy on different cycles and answer to different regulators, and ConvergX has to be credible in each on its own terms.
