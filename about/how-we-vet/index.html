Vetted by ConvergX, against a standard ConvergX owns.

By whom: ConvergX, the organisation, with its standing attached to the outcome.

Against what: whether the company is real, whether the capability is deployed rather than described, and whether anything about the situation should stop an introduction from happening at all.

Vetted, not scraped. Nothing here is assembled from public records and ranked. Someone at ConvergX reads it, asks, and decides.

What the review has to answer

01 / The standard

Every question below is answered before a company is put in front of anyone. An unanswered one stops the introduction.

Is the company real. The legal entity, who owns it, and where it operates.

Is the capability deployed, or described. Where it runs today, and who it runs for.

Is the work commercialised, at TRL 8 to 9. Research and early-stage go to a separate ConvergX arm.

Should this introduction happen at all. Ownership, control, or a conflict sitting on either side of it.

The review answers one question

02 / Scope

Should these two meet. That is the whole scope of it.

It is not a supplier qualification and does not stand in for one. First-article inspection, special-process approval and export classification are your gates, run by your people, on your programme.

What changes is who you are running them on. You enter them with a company somebody already put their name behind.

The standard does not leave when a person does

03 / Durability

A contact list leaves with the person holding it.

The standard belongs to ConvergX, is applied by ConvergX, and is recorded by ConvergX. People change. What the review has to answer does not.

ConvergX puts its name on the introduction. That name belongs to the organisation, so it is still there to answer for the decision after the people who made it have moved on.

The standard predates the platform

04 / Proof

ConvergX applied this review by hand, for years, when the whole thing lived in one organisation's working knowledge. The software records the answers. It does not supply them.
