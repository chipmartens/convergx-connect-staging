What a cheque does not move

The boundary

Three things stay where they are, at every tier, including the exclusive one.

Vetting. No tier moves a company through it faster, and no tier stands in for it.

Introductions. ConvergX decides who should meet, and both sides consent before anyone is put in front of anyone.

A sponsor is on the same footing there as a delegate who paid for one seat.

The opportunity board. A post gets onto it because ConvergX decided it belongs there, not because of what the poster spent.

That is the whole trade, stated up front. What sponsorship buys is the room knowing your name before you say it.
