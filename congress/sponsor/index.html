The nine tiers

Three categories

Nine tiers for Sep 22 to 24, 2026 in Calgary, named and priced as ConvergX sells them.

Every tier buys visibility and delegate passes. They sit in ConvergX's three sponsorship categories, in the order its own store lists them.

A delegate pass is admission for all three days. It does not carry vetted standing, an introduction, or a place on the opportunity board.

ConvergX publishes no refund, transfer or cancellation policy, so none is stated here. Ask for it in writing before a budget line is committed.

A 5 percent admin fee is added at checkout, measured on ConvergX's own cart on Jul 31, 2026.

What a cheque does not move

The boundary

Three things stay where they are, at every tier, including the exclusive one.

Vetting runs the same way at every tier, and no amount of sponsorship stands in for it.

Introductions stay with ConvergX, which decides who should meet, and both sides consent before one is made. A sponsor is on the same footing there as a delegate who paid for one seat.

A post reaches the opportunity board on ConvergX's decision that it belongs there, whatever the poster spent.
