Each of the three days does a different job.

Day one puts the requirements in the room. Day two works them. Day three is for what carries on after the building empties.

That structure is set. The programme inside it is not published yet, and nothing appears on this page until ConvergX confirms it in writing.

So this is the shape of the three days, which is the part that does not change.

What each day is for

Congress / Sep 22 to 24 / Calgary
The shape of the convening
DayIts job
Day one The room finds out what is in it. Who arrived carrying a requirement, and who arrived with something already built and already deployed.
Day two The working day. Requirements get put in front of the companies that might answer them, and ConvergX decides who should meet.
Day three Follow-through. The conversations worth a second one get set up while both parties are still in the same city.

Nothing is listed here until it is confirmed.

No speaker names, no session titles, no times, no rooms. A schedule that turns out to be wrong costs a delegate a flight change, so ConvergX publishes it once and publishes it late.
