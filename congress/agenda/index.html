Tuesday is two invite-only roundtables and the reception. The programme is Wednesday and Thursday.

ConvergX has published the schedule for all three days.

Tuesday 22 September is two invite-only executive roundtables and the welcome reception at Romero Distillery.

Wednesday 23 and Thursday 24 September are the full programme, opening at 07:30 both mornings.

There is no Panel #1. Session #1 is ConvergX-U, and the panels run from #2 through #8.

Book to land before the Tuesday reception opens at 16:00, and to leave after the Thursday close at 17:45.
