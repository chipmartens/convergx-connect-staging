Day two is the day the introductions happen.

Day one is the room finding out what is in it. Day three is for the conversations worth a second one, set up while both parties are still in the same city.

That structure is set and it is the part that does not change. The programme inside it is not published yet, and nothing appears on this page until ConvergX confirms it in writing.

Book to land before day one opens and to leave after day three closes. Delegates who fly out on the morning of day three miss the half of this that produces the follow-up.

What each day is for

DayIts job
Day one The room finds out what is in it. Who arrived carrying a requirement, and who arrived with something already built and already deployed.
Day two The working day. Requirements get put in front of the companies that might answer them, and ConvergX decides who should meet.
Day three Follow-through. The conversations worth a second one get set up while both parties are still in the same city.

Nothing is listed here until it is confirmed.
