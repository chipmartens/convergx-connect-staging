Congress app / Sep 22 to 24

Your agenda lives in the app.

So does the opportunity board, where you post what you cannot fill and read what other delegates have posted.

How to get in

ConvergX sends confirmed delegates an invitation link before the doors open. Open it on the phone you will carry in Calgary.

Where the app stops

Push and offline
  • Push notifications They reach Android phones. iPhones receive none. On an iPhone, open the app to read what has changed. The agenda and the board are the same on both.
  • Offline The agenda you already loaded still opens. Nothing new arrives until the network returns.
