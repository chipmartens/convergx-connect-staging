Why the gap stays unsaid

Find capability / 03

A capability gap is a statement about your own supply base, made in front of the people who sell into it.

Raise it in a hallway and you have told whoever was standing there.

Put it in a public solicitation and you have disclosed it to the market, on a procurement clock, with the programme attached.

So it goes quietly back to the incumbent, and turns up later as a schedule variance somebody has to report.
