A requirement your own supply base cannot fill sits with nobody. Sourcing needs a list to source from. Nobody's annual review has a line for finding a company that has never worked for you.

Where an unfillable requirement usually goes
RouteThe structural limit
A supplier database A database can only rank the companies already inside it. A company with no record has nothing for it to rank.
A government registry A registry returns keyword matches against titles written in government language. The company that can help you described itself for a different industry.
A consulting engagement A consulting engagement hands over a deck and leaves. Nobody stays attached to the names in it.
The requirement itself Naming the gap in a room full of vendors is a disclosure. So it does not get named, and it does not get worked.
