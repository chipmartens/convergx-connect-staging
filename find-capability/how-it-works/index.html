Nothing moves until a person decides.

Your post goes up with no name on it. Nothing becomes a meeting until ConvergX makes it one, and neither side learns who the other is until ConvergX releases it.

Both gates sit after the post, not in front of it. Nothing is screened on the way in, because the thing worth controlling is not what gets written down. It is who ends up in a room with whom.

ConvergX promises no timing and no match. It promises that a person reads the requirement, and that the decision is on the record.
