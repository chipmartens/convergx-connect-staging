Nothing moves until a person decides.

ConvergX approves a requirement before anyone else can see it, and releases an identity only when both sides agree to meet.

Those are the two gates, and they are what this page is about. The rest is sequence.

ConvergX makes no promise about timing and no promise of a match. It promises that a person reads the requirement, and that the decision is recorded.
