Every system you use to find a supplier is organised by industry.

So the company that already solved your problem somewhere else does not come back in any search you run. It filed itself under a different industry, and the index only reads the filing.

That is a fact about the supply base, not about the capability. A pump seal, an autonomy stack or a cold chain does not know which sector bought it first.

ConvergX convenes across industries, by hand, and has for years. The line that stops a search does not stop an introduction.

Four industries, both sides of each

Industries

Each page is written twice over: for the buyer carrying a requirement their own supply base cannot fill, and for the company that could fill it and has never been asked.

  • Aerospace and defence What it costs to qualify a company that has never made a part for the programme, against an obligation that already has a date.
  • Energy Long-lead packages committed during engineering, and an approved vendor list built from whoever supplied the last project.
  • Mining and natural resources Safety-critical parts with no replacement on the continent, and participation commitments that get audited.
  • Agriculture A harvest window with no second chance, and the engineering discipline that came out of it.
