What a new source actually costs

Aerospace and defence / 01

A new source does not clear inside a sourcing cycle.

AS9100D on the quality system. An AS9102 first article on the part. NADCAP accreditation where a special process is involved, in heat treat, chemical processing or non-destructive testing. Export control registration before a technical data package moves anywhere.

Different people run each gate, on schedules that do not line up. Flow-down means the sub-tier clears them too.

The supplier quality lead who owns this is measured on first-article pass rate and escape rate. Letting an unqualified source through the gate is the thing they are paid to prevent.

The obligation runs on its own calendar

Aerospace and defence / 02

Qualification does not get a say in it.

Both of the following are public policy, noted here as the ground ConvergX works on and not as anything ConvergX built.

Under Canada's Industrial and Technological Benefits Policy, a major defence award carries a commitment to business activity in Canada, reported against a scored contractual schedule. CPCSC sits on top of the Controlled Goods Program rather than replacing it.

The person answering for that schedule is rarely the person who owns the qualification gates. Both are measured. Neither can move the other.

So the requirement goes quietly back to the source already on the approved list. The commitment gets worked with whoever is already in the building.

The company that could have held it has never made a part for the programme. Nothing you searched was ever going to return it.
