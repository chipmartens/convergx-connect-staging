Two locks, and neither one reads the work

Two locks

A surety underwrites character, capacity and capital, and reads all three from a track record built with that surety.

A capable trade with no bonding relationship at the size of the bid does not fail on the work.

It fails because there is no history to underwrite, and a surety cannot manufacture one on demand.

The second lock is separate. General contractors and owners screen trades through third-party services that run on submitted EMR and TRIR data.

A trade new to your roster has filed neither number, and a service with no data scores nothing.

Both locks turn on a relationship the trade has not had the chance to form.
