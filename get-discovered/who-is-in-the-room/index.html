The door is the thing to check

Rigour

Rigour is what stands in for names here.

ConvergX does not publish who is in the room. A buyer states a requirement on the understanding that it stays inside the review, and a published list would end that.

What you can check is the door. There is one, ConvergX decides who comes through it, and the decision runs the same way on both sides.

That is what makes an introduction here worth an hour of your week. The company on the other end cleared the same review, run by the same organisation, and ConvergX is answerable at both ends of it.

Admission is decided, on both sides. A buyer is in the room because ConvergX approved them, on the same terms you are asking to be approved on.
