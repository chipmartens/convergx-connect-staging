Vetting is not for sale.

No payment, sponsorship or introduction changes an outcome. ConvergX checks every applicant against the same things, and a failed check stays failed.

Vetted, not scraped. ConvergX reads what you submit, looks at what you have already delivered, and decides. A problem holder is never shown a company that did not clear the review, and there is no version of this where writing a cheque skips that.

The vetting review, both sides of it
What gets checked What will not happen
Who the company is. Legal entity, ownership, and where you operate. ConvergX does not resell, repackage or list what you submit.
What you have built, and where it is running today. Nothing you post reaches a problem holder before ConvergX approves it.
Whether the capability you claim is the capability you hold. Nothing identifies you before you agree to the introduction.
Whether the work is already commercialised, at TRL 8 to 9. Early-stage and research work goes to a separate ConvergX arm, not around this review.
Certifications and accreditations, recorded as you state them. ConvergX issues no certification and validates none.

ConvergX does not sell a place in the room. A failed check is not a queue position, and there is no route around it.
