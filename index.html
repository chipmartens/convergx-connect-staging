What ConvergX is

The answer usually exists already. It sits outside the industry the requirement came from.

ConvergX asks on your behalf, vets the companies that answer, and brokers the introduction.

ConvergX stays accountable for the introduction it brokers and for who you meet.

You can ask without saying you have the question

A requirement goes out with no name attached. Nothing reaches anyone until ConvergX approves it, and your identity is released only when an introduction is brokered.

The answer can come from an industry you do not recruit from

Companies already commercialised and already selling, in energy, mining, agriculture and construction, rather than a longer version of the list you already hold.

A person decides, and the decision is on the record

Who asked, who consented, and the reason a company was ruled in or ruled out.

How a requirement reaches a company that can answer it

A qualification does not tell you whether a company is reliable.

Vetted capability matching

ConvergX ConneX is one door for a requirement you cannot fill, and one for a capability nobody has asked you for.

Finding and qualifying a counterparty you have never heard of, across industries, where a named organisation is accountable for the vetting and both sides consent before any introduction happens.

  • Find capability Where a requirement goes when your own supply base cannot fill it.
  • Get discovered Past performance is scored inside a sector, and the score does not travel.

You state the requirement, and your name is not part of it. What goes up is the work itself: what has to be made or done, to what standard, and by when.

A person at ConvergX reads it and decides who should see it. ConvergX makes a single decision, about two named organisations, and it is made before either side hears from the other.

ConvergX brokers the introduction, and the record of it is what the platform is built to keep. Who was introduced, on what basis, and who decided.

A person at ConvergX picks the counterparty, and nothing in the platform picks it for them. The decision is the product rather than a stage on the way to one.

ConvergX has brokered introductions by hand for as long as it has convened these industries. The platform is where the requirement is stated and where the record is meant to be kept.

It is built to carry a requirement and to hold the record of an introduction.
