The mark, and its floors

03 / Logo

One SVG, no fill attributes, recoloured by the surface it sits on. The hairline mark renders one tone brighter than body text: the eye resolves the gap between two thin strokes, not a solid shape.

Clear space: 12.5% of rendered width on every side. Mobile nav drops the tagline and carries the wordmark alone.

No icon-only mark exists in the supplied file. The favicon and app icon are [[PLACEHOLDER: icon-only mark decision, Kim]].

Three voices, three jobs

04 / Type

Display is the brand face at its heavier weights, tracked tight, sentence case. Semi-geometric, so it echoes the logo's circular letterforms without repeating its inline device. The logo owns caps; headlines never compete with it.

Body is the same face at its regular weight, plain and legible. One family, two registers, chosen from a six-way bakeoff.

Spec voice is the system monospace at small size with wide tracking: labels, status tags, figures, metadata. Zero download, and it is what makes annotations read as engineering rather than marketing.

Lines carry the structure

05 / Rules

The line system is taken from the logo's own construction: hairline strokes and the inline gap. Rules divide. Buttons, blocks, tags and inputs are outlined by default. Almost nothing on the site is solid, which is why a solid panel means something.

One more line exists: a rule that fades out at its end, for the tagline moment. It appears once on the whole site, on the About page, and nowhere else. It is deliberately not rendered here.

Dots carry the atmosphere

06 / Texture

The dot matrix is the primary texture, always with a density decision, an edge to terminate against, and a fade. It never sits behind body copy and it never moves.

Actions, and every state

08 / Reading

The answer can come from an industry you do not recruit from.

- Get discovered, the supply-side hook

ConvergX has matched defence and aerospace primes with suppliers for years, by hand, from a working knowledge of who can deliver. ConvergX Connect carries that work onto a platform without removing the part that matters: a person decides, and the decision is on the record.

Vetting means a named review before any introduction. ConvergX checks who a company is, what it has delivered, and whether the capability claimed is the capability held. When the introduction happens, ConvergX puts its name on it. If the counterparty is wrong, you know who to call.

Vetting is not purchasable. A solution provider cannot pay to pass, and a problem holder is never shown a company that failed review. The record of who was introduced, and on what basis, stays with ConvergX and is available to both sides of the introduction.

Warm greige on near-black, 7.32:1, clears AAA for body text. The measure caps at 68 characters.
